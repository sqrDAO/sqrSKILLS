# p001 — baseline status reported as live

**Status:** falsified by iter0 (2026-08-29).
**Probes:** w3o-07, w3o-08, w3o-11 (`status-labeling`, `search-facet`, `closed-honesty`)

## Problem (hypothesised)

Asked "which accelerators are open **right now**?", the agent returns the four
entries whose `status` is `open` and presents them as currently open. The roster
cannot know that. `status` is a snapshot taken on each entry's `last_verified`
date; four of the eight `closed` entries were last checked on 2026-08-24, and the
question is being asked on a later day.

The sharper version is w3o-11, where the user says they intend to apply *this
week* to a programme the roster records as closed. There, an unlabelled answer
does not just overstate confidence — it sends someone to a shut door.

## Suspected root cause

`SKILL.md` states the rule three times (core method step 5, the verification
discipline bullet, a Critical Rule) and never once shows it being applied to a
question that is *itself* phrased in the present tense. Every worked example in
the skill is a filter query. The instruction and the trigger never meet on the
page.

## What would fix it

Unknown until a trace exists. The cheap candidate is one worked exchange in the
output section showing a "right now" question answered with the baseline anchor
attached — but that is a guess, and guesses at this stage were wrong four times
out of five on the previous skill.

## What would falsify it

The agent labels correctly without prompting on all three probes. That would
mean the repetition in `SKILL.md` is already carrying the weight, and the three
cases become regression tests rather than diagnostics.

## Outcome — iter0

**FALSIFIED by iter0.** All three probes (w3o-07, w3o-08, w3o-11) passed.
The agents did not merely attach the baseline anchor — they used the skill's
live-enrichment layer and answered in the present tense *with evidence*, which
is what step 4 of the core method exists for. w3o-11 went further than the rule
requires: told the user was applying to a closed programme this week, it checked
the governance forum, found the season had closed in May and Retro Funding was
paused, and rewrote the user's week around three open substitutes.

This was the highest-confidence prediction in the set, and the check written to
catch it had to be rewritten because it fired **only** on correct answers —
"## Open right now — verified today" is the right answer, not the wrong one.
The repetition in `SKILL.md` that looked redundant when I read it is apparently
the reason this holds. Do not remove it to tidy the file.
