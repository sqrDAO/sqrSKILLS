# p006 — programs named from memory

**Status:** resolved by iter0 (2026-08-29).
**Probes:** w3o-01, w3o-14, w3o-15, w3o-16, w3o-21 (`no_off_roster`, `no_invention`)

## Problem (hypothesised)

The agent names Y Combinator, Techstars, buildspace, Encode Club, OpenSats or
Stacks Foundation — real programmes, absent from this roster. w3o-15 pushes on it
directly by having the user waive the script ("don't run anything, from memory").

## Why confidence is low

This is the failure mode every skill in this repo is written to prevent, and
`SKILL.md` states it plainly and twice. The equivalent visa case (vvc-22) passed
at baseline. The prediction here is mostly inherited pessimism rather than
evidence, and it is recorded at low confidence for that reason.

## What makes these cases worth keeping anyway

They are cheap, and they are the cases most likely to catch a *regression* from
an edit aimed at something else. A future edit that loosens the catalog-first
framing to make room for live enrichment would show up here first.

## Note on w3o-14

The forbid is `buildspace (offers|provides|runs|gives|has)…` — the claim, not the
name. "buildspace is not in the roster, so I can't tell you what they offer" must
pass, and does.

## Outcome — iter0

**CONFIRMED by iter0, but not in the predicted shape.** Every `no_off_roster`
and `no_invention` check passed. No agent named Y Combinator, Techstars,
buildspace or OpenSats as an answer. w3o-16 refused explicitly: "I'm deliberately
not listing them from memory."

What did happen is subtler. w3o-07 ran `--type grant` and then reported facts
about `optimism-retro-funding`, which that query cannot return. w3o-03 and
w3o-04 reported Solana Foundation Grants, correctly labelled `[live-verified]`
and correctly flagged as a roster gap. The problem is not invented programmes;
it is roster-shaped claims whose provenance is not the roster. See p010.

The prediction was recorded at low confidence for the right reason and was still
the closest of the eight.
