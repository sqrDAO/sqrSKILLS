## One-paragraph state of play

Vietnam's confirmed regulatory anchor is the **Law on Digital Technology Industry** (DTI Law), effective **1 January 2026**, which creates the first legal framework for digital assets. The government is operationalizing this via a five-year pilot program for crypto-asset trading, governed by **Resolution 05/2025/NQ-CP** and implemented via **Decision 96/QĐ-BTC**. Two new laws passed in August 2026 complete the core framework: **Law 24/2026/QH16** amends the Law on Investment to make crypto-asset services a conditional business line (effective 1 Mar 2027), and **Law 23/2026/QH16** amends the Law on AML to bring crypto service providers into the reporting perimeter (effective 1 Dec 2026). Separately, Da Nang runs a municipal controlled-trial regime under **Nghị quyết 55/2024/NQ-HĐND** (13 Dec 2024); a later **Nghị quyết 20/2026/NQ-HĐND** covers the same subject but rests on a single source. Da Nang was the first locality in the country to license a controlled trial for digital-asset technology. On **23 June 2026** the city issued **Quyết định 2728/QĐ-UBND**, promulgating a blockchain scheme to 2030 that builds a shared city chain (**DNC-Chain**) and puts crypto-asset finance in its top tier — routed to the city sandbox for crypto→fiat conversion and to the **International Financial Centre** for crypto-asset issuance and trading. That IFC is a national creation under **Nghị quyết 222/2025/QH15** and **Nghị định 323, 324 and 329/2025/NĐ-CP**. Six crypto-related trials are confirmed live in Da Nang as of 24 August 2026, and that is a floor, not a total.

### Pilot market rules (Res. 05 & Dec. 96) — CONFIRMED
- **VND Trading:** Licensed platforms will offer official trading pairs in Vietnamese đồng (VND).
- **Charter Capital:** Minimum VND 10 trillion (~US$400M) charter capital for licensed operators.
- **Foreign Ownership:** Capped at 49% for licensed operators.
- **Data Sovereignty:** Licensed exchanges are required to house their servers within Vietnam.
- **Asset-Backed Issuance:** New crypto assets issued within Vietnam must be backed by audited real-world assets.

the operators licensed national exchanges or CASPs, and do not infer that direct payment for
goods and services in USDT/USDC is now generally lawful.

**Open question — Umi Pay scope:** pre-approval filings described Umi Pay as USDT/USDC↔VND
conversion *"kết hợp tiện ích bổ trợ prediction market"*. The decision title names only
conversion plus tourism/local services. Status: UNVERIFIED.

### Da Nang blockchain scheme to 2030 (Quyết định 2728/QĐ-UBND) — EFFECTIVE / CONFIRMED

A municipal programme document, not a market rule, signed **23 June 2026**. It creates:
- **DNC-Chain** (Da Nang City Chain): a permissioned shared city chain.
- **Ten products in four tiers,** with Tier 4 for crypto-asset finance: **SP8** (crypto→fiat
  conversion) runs under the city sandbox; **SP9** (crypto-asset/RWA issuance, custody,
  trading) and **SP10** (blockchain crowdfunding) run under the IFC mechanism and are not yet
  approved or live.

### Tax treatment (Law 109, Circular 32 & 41) — CONFIRMED
- **Personal Income Tax (PIT):** **0.1% tax** on the gross value of each crypto-asset transfer for individuals, withheld at source by licensed exchanges.
- **Corporate Income Tax (CIT):** **20% CIT** on net profits from crypto activities for domestic organizations. Foreign organizations pay 0.1% on transfer revenue.
- **Value-Added Tax (VAT):** crypto-asset transfers and trading are **exempt from VAT**.

### Administrative enforcement (Decree 284) — ENACTED, EFFECTIVE 1 SEP 2026
- **Scope:** violations in the pilot market, from unlicensed services to AML.
- **Maximum fines:** VND 200 million for organizations, VND 100 million for individuals.
- **Unlicensed services or marketing:** VND 180–200 million for organizations.
- **6-month grace period for individual investors is CONFIRMED, and it is Resolution 05's, not Decree 284's.** Penalties for domestic investors trading on unlicensed platforms apply only **6 months after the first domestic crypto-asset service provider is licensed**. As no provider is yet licensed, the clock has not started.

- **Adoption context:** Vietnam consistently ranks at/near the **top of the Chainalysis Global Crypto Adoption Index**; ~**17M** estimated holders; large remittance and unbanked population driving organic usage.

## Regulators & who owns what (quick map; full detail in glossary.md)

- **National Assembly** — primary laws (Luật).
- **Government** — decrees (Nghị định) and resolutions (Nghị quyết).
- **Politburo** — disciplinary regulations for party members (Quy định).
- **Ministry of Finance (MOF)** — lead agency for the asset market + tax.
- **State Bank of Vietnam (SBV)** — payments, monetary policy, AML.
- **Ministry of Public Security (MPS)** — AML enforcement, fraud/scam crackdowns.
- **State Securities Commission (SSC)** (under MOF) — market-conduct supervision.
