## One-paragraph state of play

Vietnam's confirmed regulatory anchor is the **Law on Digital Technology Industry** (DTI Law), effective **1 January 2026**, which creates the first legal framework for digital assets. The government is operationalizing this via a five-year pilot program for crypto-asset trading, set to launch in **Q3 2026**. This pilot, governed by **Resolution 05/2025/NQ-CP** and implemented via **Decision 96/QĐ-BTC**, requires service providers to be licensed, locally incorporated, and meet high charter-capital bars. **Circulars 32/2026/TT-BTC** and **41/2026/TT-BTC** establish a tax regime, imposing a 0.1% transaction tax on individuals and a 20% CIT on corporate profits, with tax to be withheld at source by licensed exchanges. Crucially, crypto's legal recognition as property does **not** make it a lawful means of payment; the State Bank's position on this must be verified separately each run.

### Pilot market rules (as of June 2026)
- **VND Trading:** Licensed platforms will offer official trading pairs for major assets (BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC) in Vietnamese đồng (VND).
- **Asset-Backed Issuance:** Any new crypto assets issued within Vietnam must be backed by audited real-world assets.
- **Data Sovereignty:** Licensed exchanges are required to house their servers within Vietnam.
- **Restricted Access:** The government may restrict domestic retail access to certain segments of the pilot market to manage risk.

### Tax treatment (Circular 32 & 41/2026/TT-BTC)
- **Personal Income Tax (PIT):** **0.1% tax** on the gross value of each crypto-asset transfer for individuals, withheld at source by licensed exchanges.
- **Corporate Income Tax (CIT):** **20% CIT** on net profits from crypto activities for domestic organizations.
- **Value-Added Tax (VAT):** Crypto-asset transfers and trading are **exempt from VAT**.

## Regulators & who owns what (quick map; full detail in glossary.md)

- **National Assembly** — primary laws (Luật), e.g. the DTI Law.
- **Government** — decrees (Nghị định) and resolutions (Nghị quyết), e.g. Resolution 05.
- **Ministry of Finance (MOF)** — lead agency for the asset market + tax; issues circulars (Thông tư) and decisions (Quyết định).
- **State Bank of Vietnam (SBV)** — payments, monetary policy, AML; owner of the "not a means of payment" line.
- **Ministry of Public Security (MPS)** — AML enforcement, fraud/scam crackdowns.
- **General Department of Taxation** (under MOF) — tax administration, declaration/withholding.
- **State Securities Commission (SSC)** (under MOF) — market-conduct supervision.
