## One-paragraph state of play

Vietnam exited the regulatory gray zone on **1 January 2026**, when the **Law on Digital
Technology Industry (Law No. 71/2025/QH15)** took effect — the country's first comprehensive
legal recognition of digital assets as **property** (ownable, transferable, inheritable under
civil law). A five-year **pilot market** for licensed crypto-asset trading sits on top of it
(**Resolution 05/2025/NQ-CP**), with the Ministry of Finance running implementation
(**Decision 96/QĐ-BTC**) and two tax circulars now in force (**32/2026** and **41/2026/TT-BTC**).
The first licensed exchanges are targeted to go live around **Q3 2026**. Crucial nuance that
keeps getting flattened in coverage: crypto is recognized as an **asset**, not as a **means of
payment** — the State Bank's payment ban persists, and settlement must be in **VND**.

## ENACTED / EFFECTIVE (the standing framework)

| Instrument | Governs | Status | Effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Law 71/2025/QH15** — Law on Digital Technology Industry (DTI Law) | Recognizes digital assets as property; defines *digital asset* → *virtual assets* + *crypto assets*; AML/cybersecurity hooks | EFFECTIVE | 1 Jan 2026 (passed 14 Jun 2025) |
| **Resolution 05/2025/NQ-CP** | 5-year pilot for crypto-asset trading markets via licensed providers; local incorporation; VND settlement; high charter-capital bar | EFFECTIVE | Issued Sep 2025 |
| **Decision 96/QĐ-BTC** (Ministry of Finance) | Launches pilot implementation; licensing procedure for crypto-asset trading platforms | EFFECTIVE | Jan 2026 |
| **Circular 32/2026/TT-BTC** | CIT, VAT, PIT treatment of crypto-asset transactions | EFFECTIVE | 27 Mar 2026 |
| **Circular 41/2026/TT-BTC** | Tax declaration, withholding, payment, finalization for the crypto market | EFFECTIVE | 6 Apr 2026 |

### Key definitions baked into the DTI Law
- **Digital asset** — data-based asset created, issued, stored, transferred, and authenticated via digital tech in an electronic environment; recognized as property.
- **Virtual asset** — digital representation of value used for exchange/investment; **excludes** securities and fiat-based digital currency (CBDC).
- **Crypto asset** — uses encryption + distributed-ledger tech to validate transactions/ownership (e.g. BTC, ETH).

### Tax treatment (per Circulars 32 & 41/2026/TT-BTC)
- **VAT:** crypto-asset transfers/trading are **NOT** subject to VAT.
- **CIT (corporates):** **20%** on net income from crypto transfers (proceeds − purchase cost − valid transfer expenses).
- **PIT (individuals):** moving to a **securities-style 0.1% levy on gross transaction value** (tax on volume, not profit — payable even on a loss). Reported start **1 Jul 2026**; collection mechanics (withholding by licensed local exchanges vs self-declaration for foreign platforms to the tax authority) are part of the pilot. **VERIFY effective date + mechanism each run** — this is the most live tax variable.

## STANDING / BACKGROUND (unchanged but load-bearing)

- **Payment ban persists.** Crypto is **not legal tender and not a lawful means of payment.** Using it to pay is sanctionable (admin penalties under Decree 96/2014/ND-CP; reaffirmed in SBV Document 5747/NHNN-PC, 21 Jul 2017). The DTI Law's *asset* recognition does **not** override this.
- **VND settlement required** for pilot-market activity; exchanges must be **locally registered** Vietnamese companies.
- **Charter-capital bar** under the pilot is steep — on the order of **VND 10 trillion (~US$400M)** for licensed market operators (among the world's most restrictive), per commentary on Resolution 05.
- **Adoption context:** Vietnam consistently ranks at/near the **top of the Chainalysis Global Crypto Adoption Index**; ~**17M** estimated holders; large remittance and unbanked population driving organic usage.

## Regulators & who owns what (quick map; full detail in glossary.md)

- **National Assembly** — primary laws (Luật), e.g. the DTI Law.
- **Government** — decrees (Nghị định) and resolutions (Nghị quyết), e.g. Resolution 05.
- **Ministry of Finance (MOF)** — lead agency for the asset market + tax; issues circulars (Thông tư) and decisions (Quyết định); now houses securities-market supervision functions.
- **State Bank of Vietnam (SBV)** — payments, monetary policy, AML; owner of the "not a means of payment" line.
- **Ministry of Public Security (MPS)** — AML enforcement, fraud/scam crackdowns.
- **General Department of Taxation** (under MOF) — tax administration, declaration/withholding.
- **State Securities Commission (SSC)** (under MOF) — market-conduct supervision likely to extend to the regulated crypto market.
