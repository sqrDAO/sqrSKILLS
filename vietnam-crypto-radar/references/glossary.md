# Glossary — Vietnamese Legal Instruments, Regulators & Crypto Terms

Read this when you need to correctly label an instrument or explain the hierarchy.
Getting the instrument *type* right is a credibility signal — a "Thông tư" is not a "Luật."

## Legal instrument hierarchy (highest → lowest authority)

| Vietnamese | English | Issued by | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Luật** | Law | National Assembly | Top-level. e.g. **Law 71/2025/QH15** (DTI Law). The `QH15` = 15th National Assembly. |
| **Nghị quyết** | Resolution | National Assembly or Government | Policy/pilot frameworks. e.g. **Resolution 05/2025/NQ-CP** (`NQ-CP` = Government resolution). |
| **Nghị định** | Decree | Government | Implements laws; detailed rules. e.g. **Decree 284/2026/NĐ-CP**, the pilot market's administrative-penalty regime. |
| **Quyết định** | Decision | PM, Government, or a Minister | Specific actions/approvals. e.g. **Decision 96/QĐ-BTC** (`QĐ-BTC` = MOF decision). |
| **Thông tư** | Circular | A Ministry | Technical/operational detail. e.g. **Circular 32/2026/TT-BTC** (`TT-BTC` = MOF circular). |
| **Công văn / Văn bản** | Official Letter / Document | Any body | Guidance/position, not a regulation per se. e.g. SBV Document 5747/NHNN-PC. |
| **Đề án** | Scheme / programme document | Attached to a Quyết định | Not an instrument in its own right — it has force only through the Decision that promulgates it, and you cite that Decision. e.g. Da Nang's blockchain scheme to 2030, issued with **Quyết định 2728/QĐ-UBND**. A Đề án allocates work and budget; it does not create rights or licences. |

Local instruments use the same grammar: `-HĐND` = Hội đồng nhân dân (a People's Council,
which legislates for a province or city) and `-UBND` = Ủy ban nhân dân (its People's
Committee, which executes). So `55/2024/NQ-HĐND` is a city council resolution and
`2728/QĐ-UBND` a city committee decision. Both are municipal: they bind inside that city
only, and they never create a national licence. Councils in different provinces number
independently, so always match the issuing body, not just the number.

**Reading a number:** `32/2026/TT-BTC` = circular no. 32, year 2026, issued by Bộ Tài chính
(Ministry of Finance). The agency suffix tells you who owns it:
- `-CP` = Chính phủ (Government) · `-QH15` = Quốc hội (National Assembly, 15th)
- `-BTC` = Bộ Tài chính (Ministry of Finance) · `-NHNN` = Ngân hàng Nhà nước (State Bank)
- `-BCA` = Bộ Công an (Ministry of Public Security)

## Regulators

- **Quốc hội (National Assembly)** — passes Laws and top resolutions.
- **Chính phủ (Government)** — decrees and resolutions; executive implementation.
- **Bộ Tài chính / Ministry of Finance (MOF)** — lead agency for the crypto-asset market and tax; issues the operative circulars/decisions; now also oversees securities-market functions.
- **Ngân hàng Nhà nước / State Bank of Vietnam (SBV)** — payments, monetary policy, AML; source of the "crypto is not a means of payment" position.
- **Bộ Công an / Ministry of Public Security (MPS)** — AML enforcement, fraud and scam crackdowns.
- **Tổng cục Thuế / General Department of Taxation** — under MOF; tax declaration, withholding, finalization.
- **Ủy ban Chứng khoán Nhà nước / State Securities Commission (SSC)** — under MOF; market-conduct supervision, plausibly extending to the regulated crypto market.

### Local and IFC bodies (Da Nang)

- **HĐND thành phố Đà Nẵng (People's Council)** — passes the city resolutions (`NQ-HĐND`) that create the municipal controlled-trial regime.
- **UBND thành phố Đà Nẵng (People's Committee)** — issues the trial approvals and city schemes (`QĐ-UBND`).
- **Sở Khoa học và Công nghệ (Department of Science and Technology)** — the city's lead agency: it guides and supervises each controlled trial and leads the blockchain scheme.
- **IFC Management Board and its Da Nang office** — the governance layer for Vietnam's International Financial Centre under Nghị định 323/2025/NĐ-CP. Distinct from the city's own departments, and distinct again from the World Bank's IFC.

## Crypto / domain terms

- **Digital asset (tài sản số)** — umbrella category recognized as property under the DTI Law.
- **Virtual asset (tài sản ảo)** — value representation for exchange/investment; excludes securities and CBDC.
- **Crypto asset (tài sản mã hóa)** — encryption + DLT-based (BTC, ETH, etc.).
- **VASP** — Virtual Asset Service Provider; the licensed-operator concept under the pilot.
- **The pilot** — the 5-year national regulated-market programme under Resolution 05/2025/NQ-CP. Say "the pilot", not "the sandbox", for this one.
- **Sandbox / thử nghiệm có kiểm soát** — ambiguous on its own; there are three. (1) Da Nang's **municipal** controlled-trial regime, which grants time-limited trial certificates by QĐ-UBND. Nghị quyết 55/2024/NQ-HĐND is the resolution the 2025 approvals were issued under; a later 20/2026/NQ-HĐND covers the same subject, and which one governs the August 2026 batch is unresolved — see `baseline.md`. (2) The **national** pilot above. (3) The **IFC**'s controlled-testing environment for new financial models under Nghị định 323/2025/NĐ-CP. They have different issuers, perimeters and legal effects; never let a source's bare "sandbox" carry across them.
- **DTI Law** — the Law on Digital Technology Industry (Law 71/2025/QH15).
- **Decree 284** — Decree 284/2026/NĐ-CP, the administrative-penalty regime for crypto assets and the pilot market; enacted 16 July 2026 and effective 1 September 2026.
- **IFC (Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế)** — Vietnam's International Financial Centre, created by Nghị quyết 222/2025/QH15 and Nghị định 323/2025/NĐ-CP: **one entity at two sites, Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang**. ⚠️ **Name collision:** the World Bank Group's International Finance Corporation is also "IFC" and also appears in Da Nang coverage, sometimes in the same article. Read which one a source means before repeating it; a cooperation MoU with the World Bank's IFC is not a Vietnam-IFC admission.
- **RWA (tài sản thực được token hóa)** — real-world assets tokenised on-chain. Da Nang's blockchain scheme puts an RWA issuance/custody/trading platform (SP9) in its top tier, routed to the Vietnam IFC rather than the city sandbox.
- **DNC-Chain (Da Nang City Chain)** — Da Nang's planned shared permissioned city blockchain, positioned as a "digital trust layer". By its own scheme it will not host a crypto-asset exchange and does not carry transaction detail for crypto-asset models — only verification and compliance evidence.
- **Đề án vs Quyết định** — see the hierarchy table: cite the Decision number, describe the Đề án's content.
- **Charter capital (vốn điều lệ)** — registered capital; the pilot's ~VND 10tn bar is a charter-capital requirement.
- **VND settlement** — the requirement that pilot-market transactions settle in Vietnamese dong.

## Three distinctions to never conflate

1. **Recognized as property** — YES since 1 Jan 2026 (ownable/transferable/inheritable).
2. **Lawful means of payment** — NO (SBV ban persists; paying in crypto is sanctionable).
3. **Tradable on a licensed market** — only via pilot-licensed providers; offshore-exchange use is outside the protected/regulated perimeter.

## Status labels used by this skill

- **EFFECTIVE / CONFIRMED** — verified through a Tier-1 source or equivalent legal text.
- **ENACTED** — signed/issued, but may not yet be effective.
- **DRAFT / PROPOSED** — published for consultation or announced as planned, not yet binding.
- **EXPECTED** — official or high-quality-source timeline, but no enacted instrument yet.
- **REPORTED** — credible media/analyst signal; needs confirmation before being treated as law.
- **NEEDS_PRIMARY_SOURCE** — an older baseline or secondary claim exists, but the current agent must verify it on a primary legal database, official ministry page, or two independent named Tier-2 sources before presenting it as fact.
- **RUMORED** — market chatter only; do not include as fact.
