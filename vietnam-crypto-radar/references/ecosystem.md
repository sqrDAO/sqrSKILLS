# Vietnam Web3 Ecosystem — Players & Communities Map

> **LAST VERIFIED: 19 June 2026.**
> This is a context map of WHO is active in Vietnam's Web3 scene — communities, builders,
> education programs, events, and flagship projects. It is **separate from the regulatory
> baseline** (`baseline.md`); it is not part of the enacted-vs-draft diff loop. Read it when
> the user asks who the players/communities/events are, not for a legal update.
>
> Verification note: ecosystem facts are mostly Tier-2 / Tier-3 sourced (community pages,
> event sites, crypto media) and are **time-sensitive** — programs, cohorts, and events come
> and go. Re-verify status and dates live before relying on them. Status labels:
> `Active` (currently running), `Reported` (cited but unconfirmed this run), `Defunct`
> (shut down or irrelevant — do not resurface).

This is a map, not an application tracker. Opportunity-shaped entries (apply-able hackathons,
education programs, bounties) are catalogued and queryable in the **`web3-opportunities`**
skill's JSON roster — this file is the narrative context and cross-links to it.

## Where the community organizes

- **Primary channels:** X (KOLs, builders, project announcements), Telegram (trading,
  airdrops, news), Discord (project servers), and ecosystem hubs (sqrDAO, Superteam, Open
  Guild). Most projects begin on X Spaces or Discord, then mint/launch on global platforms.
- **No active Vietnam-specific Web3 subreddit.** Reddit searches for VN Web3 return off-topic
  r/VietNam content (travel, offshoring, etc.); do not rely on Reddit for this ecosystem.
- **Geographic hub:** Da Nang has become the physical gathering point (W3BS, Unchained, DAVAS
  all clustered there in 2026), alongside Ho Chi Minh City's ~5,000 active Web3 developers.
