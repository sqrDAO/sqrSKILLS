## 1. Entity types and when each fits

- **Cong ty TNHH mot thanh vien (single-member LLC).** One owner (individual or
  organization). Simplest governance: the owner decides everything; the charter
  and owner's decisions are the governing instruments. Liability limited to
  charter capital.
- **Cong ty TNHH hai thanh vien tro len (multi-member LLC, 2 to 50 members).**
  Governed by a Members' Council (Hoi dong thanh vien). Adds Members' Council resolutions as a governance layer;
  member votes are weighted by capital share.
- **Cong ty co phan (joint-stock company, JSC).** For 3+ shareholders, share
  issuance, ESOP, or raising from many investors. Heavier governance (General
  Meeting of Shareholders, Board of Management, sometimes a Supervisory Board).
- **Decision guide.** Solo founder wanting simplicity: single-member LLC.
  Bringing in a co-founder or a small number of partners with fixed stakes:
  multi-member LLC. Planning priced equity rounds, an ESOP pool, or many
  cap-table entries: JSC. A crypto token raise does not by itself require a JSC;
  use `web3-legal-agreements` when available for that structuring, or identify
  the questions for qualified counsel.

## 2. The core corporate documents

- **Giay chung nhan dang ky doanh nghiep (GCN DKDN / Enterprise Registration
  Certificate).** The company's identity card: tax code, legal name (VN and
  foreign), head office, charter capital, members and their shares, legal
  representative, business lines. Issued and amended by the provincial So Tai
  chinh. Every change to these fields is a registration event.
- **Dieu le cong ty (Company Charter).** The internal constitution: purpose, name,
  head office, business lines, charter capital and member shares, rights and
  duties of members/owner, Members' Council mechanics, the legal representative's
  powers, profit distribution, capital increase/decrease, reorganization,
  dissolution. Must be internally consistent with the GCN DKDN. When the GCN
  changes (new member, new capital, new legal rep), the charter is usually amended
  in step.
- **Giay de nghi dang ky doanh nghiep (Enterprise Registration Application).** The
  application form used at formation and, in updated forms, for certain changes.
  Follow the current Decree 168/2025/ND-CP form set.
- Supporting instruments: list of members / authorized representatives, minutes
  and resolutions of the Members' Council, seal-specimen notice.

## 3. Business-registration changes (what triggers a filing)

A filing with the So Tai chinh (via the National Business Registration Portal) is
required to change any of: company name, head-office address, charter capital,
business lines, legal representative, members or their capital shares,
company-type conversion, or setting up/closing a branch or representative office.

Standard change package (adapt to the specific change):
1. **Thong bao thay doi noi dung dang ky doanh nghiep** (Notice of change), on the
   current form.
2. The **decision and minutes** of the competent body (owner's decision for a
   single-member LLC; Members' Council resolution and minutes for a multi-member
   LLC).
3. The **amended charter** if the change touches charter content.
4. Supporting documents specific to the change (for example a capital-transfer
   contract for a member/share change, a PIT declaration for a capital transfer).
5. **Beneficial-owner list** if this is the entity's first registered change after
   1 July 2025.

Route filings through an external corporate-services / law firm. Prepare a
clean, consistent package; do not attempt to submit.

## 4. Capital contribution transfer

**Hop dong chuyen nhuong phan von gop** (capital-contribution transfer contract)
moves an ownership stake between people. Core clauses:
- Transferor and transferee identity (name, CCCD, address as blanks).
- The stake: VND amount and % of charter capital, referencing the GCN DKDN.
- Transfer price and payment mechanics; whether payment is made at signing before
  the company's legal representative.
- Transferee assumes all rights and obligations of a member from completion.
- Dispute resolution: negotiation first, then the competent court.
- Made in enough originals for both parties, the company file, and the registry.

**Tax link:** a capital transfer by an individual triggers a PIT declaration
(To khai thue TNCN cho chuyen nhuong von, historically form 04/CNV-TNCN). PIT on
capital transfer is on the gain (transfer price minus cost minus expenses); a
transfer at cost yields zero gain and zero PIT but the declaration is still filed.
Route the actual declaration to the accountant.

## 5. Adding or changing a member (single to multi-member conversion)

Bringing a second member into a single-member LLC converts it to a multi-member
LLC. This is a **company-type conversion**, not just a member change, and it must
be registered within the statutory window from when the new member commits
capital. The package layers a conversion notice onto the standard change package,
plus a new multi-member charter and an updated member list. If the user provides a worked example, update all figures and the address format
before adapting it.

## 6. Representative office and branch

- **Van phong dai dien (representative office).** A dependent unit that may
  promote, coordinate, and represent, but generally may **not** conduct
  direct revenue-generating business on its own. Registered with its own operation
  certificate and a head (nguoi dung dau). Good for local presence, events, and partner relations without standing
  up a separate taxable business.
- **Chi nhanh (branch).** A dependent unit that **may** carry on the company's
  business lines and generate revenue; heavier tax and accounting footprint.
- Choose the rep office when the need is presence and coordination; choose the
  branch when the local unit must invoice and trade in its own right.

## 7. Seal, power of attorney, chief-accountant appointment

- **Con dau (company seal).** The company decides its seal form and manages its
  use; the legal representative is responsible for it. A seal-specimen notice is
  filed when adopting or changing the seal.
- **Giay uy quyen (power of attorney).** Authorizes a named person to act for the
  company or the legal representative within a defined scope and period (for
  example, to lodge and collect registration results). Keep scope and duration
  explicit and narrow.
- **Quyet dinh bo nhiem Ke toan truong (chief-accountant appointment decision).**
  An internal decision by the Director appointing the chief accountant, effective
  from the stated date, citing the Enterprise Law and the charter. Names the
  appointee (leave CCCD as a blank), the effective date, and the reporting line.

## 8. Beneficial owner

Under the amended Enterprise Law and Decree 168/2025/ND-CP, the company must
identify and keep current its beneficial owner(s): an individual who ultimately
owns/controls, generally via >= 25% ownership or via control rights. For an entity
formed before 1 July 2025, the BO list is submitted at the **next registered
change**. Prepare a BO list to accompany the next change package; the practical
inputs are the member list and any control arrangements. Route the actual
determination through counsel if control is exercised through anything other than
straight equity.

## 9. Dissolution

Voluntary dissolution runs through an owner's decision or Members' Council
resolution, creditor and tax clearance, closing the tax code, returning the seal,
and deregistration. This is a multi-step, tax-heavy process; scope the documents
here but route execution to the accountant and the corporate-services firm.

## 10. Review checklist

When reviewing a corporate document:
- [ ] Legal name matches the current GCN DKDN exactly (VN, foreign, abbreviated).
- [ ] Tax code (MST) present and correct; head office in two-tier address format.
- [ ] Legal representative name and title correct; single named legal rep unless
      the charter allows more.
- [ ] Charter capital and member shares in the document reconcile with the GCN
      DKDN and with each other (percentages sum to 100).
- [ ] "Can cu" chain uses the current Enterprise Law + 76/2025 amendment and
      Decree 168/2025 (not 01/2021), and the 2015 Civil Code (not 2005).
- [ ] Registry named as So Tai chinh, not So Ke hoach va Dau tu.
- [ ] Change package includes the competent-body decision/minutes and, if content
      changes, the amended charter.
- [ ] Beneficial-owner list included if this is the first change post 1 July 2025.
- [ ] Personal data left as blanks, not copied from another document.
- [ ] Ends with the not-a-lawyer line and the handoff to the filing firm.
