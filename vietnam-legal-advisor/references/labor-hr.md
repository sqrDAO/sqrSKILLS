## 1. Contract types

The Labor Code recognizes **only two** labor-contract types now (the old seasonal
/ under-12-month contract was abolished):
- **Hop dong lao dong khong xac dinh thoi han** (indefinite-term).
- **Hop dong lao dong xac dinh thoi han** (definite-term, up to 36 months).

Key mechanics:
- Substance over form: if an agreement shows paid work under one party's
  management and supervision, it is a labor contract regardless of what it is
  titled. This is the hook behind the contractor-vs-employee risk (section 7).
- A definite-term contract can be renewed **once**; a further continuation
  generally makes it indefinite. If a definite-term contract expires and the
  employee keeps working without a new contract signed within **30 days**, it
  becomes indefinite-term.
- A labor contract must be in writing, in two copies, one per party (electronic
  form is allowed under conditions).
- **Phu luc hop dong (contract annex).** May detail or modify content but **cannot
  change the contract term**. A salary change is typically done by annex; use
  a supplied salary-raise annex as a pattern if available.

## 2. Probation

- Probation can be a clause in the labor contract or a separate probation
  agreement.
- Maximum probation: **180 days for enterprise managers**, 60 days for roles
  needing a college degree or higher, 30 days for roles needing intermediate
  qualification, 6 working days for other roles.
- Probation wage must be at least 85% of the job wage.
- On passing probation, the employer must conclude the labor contract.

## 3. Working time and leave

- Normal working time: up to 8 hours/day and **48 hours/week**; the state
  encourages a 40-hour week.
- Overtime: capped at 50% of normal daily hours per day, **40 hours per month**,
  and 200 hours/year (300 in specified cases), with overtime premium rates
  (150% weekday, 200% weekly rest day, 300% public holiday).
- Paid annual leave: **12 days/year** baseline for standard conditions, rising
  with seniority and for arduous work.
- Public holidays and paid-leave events are per the Code and any better company
  policy.

## 4. Wages and payslip mechanics

- Wage stated in the contract must be at least the applicable **regional minimum
  wage** (Region I VND 5,310,000 from 1 Jan 2026; region set by workplace
  location, see the updates file).
- Pay method and cycle are stated in the contract (example: bank
  transfer, monthly, on a fixed day).
- The contract wage plus fixed, regular allowances forms the base for both PIT and
  social-insurance contributions; auditors scrutinize allowances excluded from the
  base.

## 5. Termination and severance

Termination is where Vietnamese labor law is strict; get the ground and the notice
right.

- **Cases of contract termination (Art. 34):** expiry, completion of the work,
  mutual agreement, lawful unilateral termination by either side, disciplinary
  dismissal, and others.
- **Employer unilateral termination (Art. 36)** is allowed only on specified
  grounds (for example the employee repeatedly fails to perform, prolonged
  illness, force majeure/downsizing after the required steps, unauthorized absence
  of 5 consecutive working days, or providing false information at hiring), and
  requires the statutory **advance notice** (generally 45 days indefinite-term,
  30 days definite-term, 3 working days for short contracts; industry variations
  exist).
- **Prohibited-timing rule (Art. 37):** cannot unilaterally terminate an employee
  who is on sick/annual leave, pregnant, on maternity leave, or raising a child
  under 12 months, among others.
- **Illegal termination consequences:** reinstatement, back pay of wages and
  insurance for the no-work period, plus at least 2 months' wages; if notice was
  short, pay for the un-notified days.
- **Severance allowance (tro cap thoi viec, Art. 46):** for an employee with at
  least 12 months of regular service on qualifying terminations, **half a month's
  wage per year of service**, with the period covered by unemployment insurance
  excluded from the count (so for fully UI-covered employment, statutory severance
  is often minimal).
- **Timing:** settle termination payments and return documents within **14 working
  days** (extendable to 30 in limited cases).

Always identify the exact termination ground and the matching notice period before
drafting a termination letter; a mismatch is the usual cause of an illegal-
termination finding.

## 6. Social insurance and payroll contributions

Under the Social Insurance Law 2024 (from 1 July 2025):
- Total social contribution is **32% of the contribution base**: employer
  **21.5%** (SI 17.5%, HI 3%, UI 1%), employee **10.5%** (SI 8%, HI 1.5%, UI 1%).
- **UI is Vietnamese-only.** Foreign employees with valid work permits pay SI and
  HI but not UI.
- Contribution cap for SI and HI: **VND 46,800,000/month** (20x reference level,
  2026).
- Trade-union fund: employer 2% of payroll; employee 1% capped at VND 180,000
  where a grassroots union exists.
- **Directors / legal representatives paid by dividend rather than salary** fall
  under a different contribution arrangement; do not assume standard payroll
  contributions apply to an owner-manager taking dividends. Confirm with the
  accountant.
- Late SI registration/payment carries daily late-payment charges; register new
  hires on time.

## 7. Employee vs contractor (the classification line)

This is the single highest-risk HR issue for a founder-run company that uses
service contracts (HDDV) for ongoing roles.

- A **service contract (HDDV)** with an individual is a commercial engagement:
  no social insurance, and PIT handled by **10% withholding** on payments over
  VND 2,000,000 (see `tax-compliance.md`). It is appropriate for genuinely
  independent, project-scoped, self-directed work.
- If the reality is ongoing, directed, supervised, fixed-schedule work at the
  company's place, the relationship can be **deemed a labor contract** regardless
  of the title. Consequences of a re-characterization: back social insurance,
  back PIT with a 20% penalty on underpaid tax plus 0.03%/day interest, plus labor
  obligations (leave, notice, severance) reverting to statutory defaults, and the
  worker gaining employee claims.
- Practical test before choosing HDDV over a labor contract: Is the work
  project-scoped with a deliverable, or a standing role? Does the person set their
  own method and hours, or follow the company's schedule and supervision? Do they
  serve other clients? The more it looks like a standing, supervised role, the more
  it should be a labor contract.
- If a supplied HDDV service contract covers a standing function
  (for example business development), flag it as classification-risk when
  reviewing, and offer the labor-contract alternative rather than silently
  blessing the HDDV.

## 8. Internal labor rules and other required HR documents

- **Noi quy lao dong (internal labor rules).** Required and must be registered
  once the company reaches the statutory headcount threshold; covers working time,
  order, safety, protection of company assets and secrets, and the discipline
  process. Disciplinary dismissal is only defensible if the conduct is a
  registered rule breach handled through the proper process.
- Job description, probation agreement, labor contract, contract annex, and (for
  managers taking dividends) the corporate appointment decision round out the
  standard HR file.

## 9. Review checklist

- [ ] Contract is one of the two valid types; term and renewal handled correctly.
- [ ] Probation length within the cap for the role; probation wage >= 85%.
- [ ] Contract wage >= applicable regional minimum wage (2026 figures).
- [ ] Salary/insurance base and allowance treatment consistent.
- [ ] Termination ground cited maps to Art. 34/36 and carries the correct notice;
      prohibited-timing checked.
- [ ] Severance math uses half-month-per-year with the UI-covered period excluded.
- [ ] Insurance clauses reflect the 2024 SI Law rates and caps, not older ones.
- [ ] If an HDDV is being used for a standing role, classification risk flagged
      and the labor-contract alternative offered.
- [ ] Personal data left as blanks.
- [ ] Ends with the not-a-lawyer line; payroll/insurance filings routed to the
      accountant.
