# Contract Execution and Close-Out: Nghiem Thu, Phu Luc, Thanh Toan

> UNVERIFIED imported reference/template. Legal claims, figures, dates, and
> citations require current primary-source verification before use, as described
> in SKILL.md. Examples and document-style defaults are not universal legal rules.

Everything after signature. A Vietnamese service contract does not pay out on
goodwill: each milestone has to be requested, minuted, evidenced, and only then
invoiced. Most of the money risk in a consulting engagement sits in this chain,
not in the contract that started it.

Read this whenever the user says nghiem thu, bien ban, phu luc, dieu chinh gia
tri, de nghi thanh toan, cong van, khau tru, quyet toan, or asks how to close out
a milestone, claim a payment, or respond to a counterparty's acceptance form.

## 1. The four-document chain and why the order matters

For one milestone under one contract, the normal sequence is:

| # | Document | Function | Signed by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `THU DE NGHI NGHIEM THU` | Provider asks the client to inspect and accept | Provider only |
| 2 | `DANH MUC HO SO NGHIEM THU` | Line-by-line reconciliation of scope against files delivered | Provider, with tick boxes for the client |
| 3 | `BIEN BAN NGHIEM THU` | The acceptance itself: what is accepted, what is defective, what it is worth | Both parties, sealed |
| 4 | `PHU LUC ...` (only if value changes) | Amends the contract value or scope | Both parties, sealed |
| 5 | `CONG VAN DE NGHI THANH TOAN` | Asks for the tranche, net of whatever items 3 and 4 settled | Provider only |

Documents 1, 2 and 3 travel together as one package. Document 4 is a contract
instrument and lives with the contract. Document 5 is a letter and travels alone
(see section 11).

**The chain is directional and the dates must follow it.** A payment request
cites the minutes; the minutes cite the amendment; the amendment cites the
contract and the delivery result. A document may never cite an annex dated after
itself. If the drafting happens out of order, re-date the downstream document,
never backdate the upstream one.

## 2. Thu de nghi nghiem thu (acceptance request letter)

One or two pages, provider-signed, Vietnamese only where the counterparty is
Vietnamese. What it must carry:

- A field table at the top: `Kinh gui` (client legal name), `Ben de nghi`,
  `Hop dong` (number and signing date), `Hang muc` (the milestone and its date),
  `Can cu` (the exact clauses that create the acceptance right and the milestone).
- A short request paragraph asking the client to inspect the file set and confirm
  the milestone against the scope, deliverables and acceptance criteria in the
  scope appendix.
- **The carve-outs, stated in the letter itself**, not left to be discovered in
  the minutes: any line the provider is not claiming, any line where the client
  used a different solution, any line where the provider supplied a product but
  did not operate it on site.
- A `Tai lieu kem theo` table listing each attached document and its purpose.
- **The response window**, quoted from the contract: "Ben A phan hoi trong 05
  ngay lam viec ke tu khi nhan du ho so", with the clause number.
- A sentence saying that signing does not discharge the surviving obligations
  (editable source files, data handover, post-programme report, confidentiality,
  IP, latent defects). Put this in the letter and again in the minutes.
- A named focal point.

## 3. Bien ban nghiem thu (acceptance minutes)

The instrument that actually matters. Numbered `So: [nn]/[YYYY]/BBNT/[ABBR-ABBR]`.
Structure that has worked:

```
[QUOC HIEU BLOCK]
BIEN BAN NGHIEM THU HANG MUC TAI [PLACE]
[Programme name] · [the clause that requires a bien ban]
So: 01/2026/BBNT/[A]-[B] · Ngay lap: dd/mm/yyyy · [Milestone, venue, date]

- Can cu Bo luat Dan su so 91/2015/QH13 ... va Luat Thuong mai so 36/2005/QH11 ...;
- Can cu Hop dong dich vu so [...] ngay [...] giua [A] va [B];
- Can cu pham vi, san pham ban giao va tieu chi nghiem thu tai Phu luc 01 cua Hop dong;
- Can cu ket qua trien khai [milestone] ngay [...].

Hom nay, ngay ...... thang ...... nam ......, tai [place], chung toi gom:

DIEU 1. THONG TIN CAC BEN            (table: legal name, MST, address, representative, title)
DIEU 2. PHAM VI DE NGHI NGHIEM THU   (table: STT | hang muc | khoi luong doi chieu | co so xac nhan)
DIEU 3. KET QUA NGHIEM THU           (tick boxes + any narrative findings)
DIEU 4. KHIEM KHUYET VA YEU CAU KHAC PHUC  (table: item | tinh trang | yeu cau tiep theo + deadline)
DIEU 5. GIA TRI NGHIEM THU VA NGHIA VU THANH TOAN
DIEU 6. HIEU LUC VA BAN LUU
[SIGNATURE + SEAL BLOCK, both parties]
```

Points that carry weight:

- **Dieu 2 is the claim, so it must also carry the exclusions.** Write the
  negative next to the positive in the same row: "Khong bao gom viec tim kiem, de
  xuat hoac thu xep nguon dien gia". A line item that is silent about what it
  excludes will be read as claiming everything.
- **Dieu 3 offers a real choice, not a formality.** Three tick boxes: full
  acceptance, conditional acceptance, refusal. If conditional is ticked, Dieu 4
  has to say what fixes it.
- **Conditional acceptance with no dated remediation is an open-ended
  liability.** This is the trap. If the minutes record a defect, take a deduction
  for it, and then write "chua yeu cau khac phuc tai thoi diem ky", the provider
  has paid for the defect and still owes an undated obligation. Either close the
  item outright ("khong con yeu cau xu ly", because the deduction settles it) or
  give it a date. Never leave both open.
- **Dieu 5 links acceptance to money in four moves**: (5.1) which tranche this
  milestone triggers and its contractual percentage and amount; (5.2) the
  deduction and the instrument that authorises it; (5.3) **the conditionality
  clause**; (5.4) the payment term, the invoice trigger, and where pass-through
  costs settle.
- **The conditionality clause is the single most useful sentence in the set.**
  Write it every time value is adjusted:

  > Muc 5.2 co hieu luc khi Phu luc [nn] duoc hai Ben cung ky; neu chua ky, gia
  > tri Dot [n] giu nguyen theo Hop dong va phan chenh lech duoc xu ly khi Phu
  > luc [nn] co hieu luc.

  It lets the minutes be signed today without the deduction becoming a gift if
  the amendment is never countersigned.
- **Dieu 6 carries deemed acceptance**: if the client does not respond in writing
  within the contractual window, acceptance is handled per the response clause.
  Also state that signing does not waive confidentiality, IP, data, latent
  defects, or remediation obligations.
- Made in `02 ban` of equal value, one per party, both signed and sealed.

## 4. Danh muc ho so nghiem thu (acceptance file index)

A reconciliation table, not a cover sheet. Four sections:

1. **Doi chieu theo Phu luc 01**: one row per scope line, with the files that
   evidence it and, in the last column, exactly what confirmation is still needed
   from the client. This column is what converts a pile of files into a
   signable claim.
2. **Cau truc bo ho so**: the folder structure and the file count per folder.
3. **Gioi han cua ho so**: what the package deliberately does not contain
   (internal trackers, contracts, budgets, payment records, cost evidence,
   personal data) and what it does not replace (source files, app data, the
   post-programme report).
4. **Xac nhan ho so**: tick boxes for the client, whether the file set was
   received in full, whether further operational evidence is requested, and
   whether the package is ready for the minutes to be signed.

**One sentence belongs in every index**: "Viec mot tai lieu co trong bo ho so
khong chung minh tai lieu do da duoc gui, su dung hoac nghiem thu." Presence is
not proof of delivery, and an index that implies otherwise will be quoted back.

## 5. Phu luc (contract amendment)

**Any change to agreed value, scope or schedule is an amendment**, drafted under
the contract's own amendment clause and signed by both parties. It is a part of
the contract ("bo phan khong tach roi cua Hop dong"), not correspondence.

```
[QUOC HIEU BLOCK]
PHU LUC [nn] BO SUNG HOP DONG
[one-line subject: Dieu chinh gia tri Hop dong sau nghiem thu moc ...]
Hop dong dich vu so [...] · Ngay lap: dd/mm/yyyy

- Can cu Hop dong dich vu so [...] ngay [...] va Phu luc [previous] ...;
- Can cu Dieu [n] cua Hop dong ve sua doi, bo sung Hop dong;
- Can cu ket qua trien khai [milestone] va Bien ban nghiem thu ngay [...].

DIEU 1..n  one Dieu per adjusted line, each with:
             n.1 Pham vi thuc te  (what actually happened, in facts)
             n.2 Muc giam         (pre-VAT amount, VAT amount, total)
DIEU n+1. GIA TRI HOP DONG SAU DIEU CHINH   (table: truoc dieu chinh | sau dieu chinh)
DIEU n+2. AP DUNG VAO LICH THANH TOAN
DIEU n+3. HIEU LUC
[SIGNATURE + SEAL BLOCK, both parties]
```

Rules learned the hard way:

- **One Phu luc per negotiation, not per adjustment.** Several related
  adjustments arising from one milestone belong in a single document the client
  signs once. A standalone price proposal that overlaps an amendment should be
  folded in as its own Dieu rather than sent separately.
- **Separate a permanent reduction from an acceptance-driven one, in the
  reasoning if not in the document.** A line the provider never performed is a
  permanent scope reduction under the amendment clause. A line performed below
  standard is a one-off acceptance deduction under the acceptance clause. They
  have different consequences for the remaining tranches, so say which is which
  in each Dieu even when both sit in the same Phu luc.
- **State the no-double-counting principle explicitly** where an adjusted line
  overlaps another priced line: "Nguyen tac khong tinh trung. [Item] da duoc tinh
  tai [other line], khong chuyen thanh [new fee]." Removing a line for
  non-performance and re-charging the same work under a different heading is the
  error this sentence prevents.
- **Each Dieu states the facts before the number.** `Pham vi thuc te` first,
  `Muc giam` second. An amendment that opens with an amount reads as a haircut;
  one that opens with what happened reads as a reconciliation.
- The closing Dieu must say the rest of the contract and prior annexes are
  unchanged, and that signing does not waive any other right.

## 6. Cong van de nghi thanh toan (payment request)

**Short.** One page of letter, plus the signature page if the signing space needs
it. It is a letter, not an instrument: it does not amend anything, it does not
argue, and it does not re-open the acceptance.

Where an executed request already exists for an earlier tranche, **follow that
original exactly**, including its masthead layout and its typographic quirks. Its
form is the form the client's accounts department has already paid against.

```
[Issuer name, left]                      [QUOC HIEU BLOCK, right]
So:      /DNTT                           [Place], ngay ... thang ... nam ...
V/v: Thanh toan dot [n] gia tri Hop dong dich vu so [...]

Kinh gui: [CLIENT LEGAL NAME IN FULL]

  [courtesy opening]
  Can cu Hop dong dich vu so [...] ngay [...] ... ;
  Can cu Bien ban nghiem thu ... ngay ... ;
  Can cu Phu luc [nn] bo sung Hop dong ngay ... ,
  [Provider] de nghi Quy Cong ty xem xet thanh toan dot [n] theo khoan [...]:

   1. Gia tri Hop dong tam tinh sau dieu chinh:      [amount] dong
      1.1 Phi dich vu co dinh sau dieu chinh:        [amount] dong
      1.2 Chi phi ben thu ba (chi ho):               [amount] dong
   2. Gia tri de nghi thanh toan dot [n]:            [amount] dong
      2.1 Dot [n] theo khoan [...]:                  [amount] dong
      2.2 Giam tru theo Phu luc [nn]:              - [amount] dong

Bang chu: [amount in words] dong.
Thoi han thanh toan: ... theo khoan [...].
Ve hoa don: ... theo khoan [...].
[bank account, bank, beneficiary + MST]
[closing line]
Noi nhan:            [ISSUER]
- Nhu tren;          GIAM DOC
- Luu VP; KT.        (Ky, ghi ro ho ten va dong dau)
                     [NAME]
```

- **Two numbered blocks only**: the adjusted contract value, and the amount
  claimed with its one deduction line. Every reason for the deduction lives in
  the Phu luc; the letter carries the net effect and a citation.
- `Bang chu` must match the digits exactly. Compute it, do not type it.
- **No running header and no page footer** if the executed original has none.
- Keep third-party pass-through amounts visible but outside the fee arithmetic.

## 7. Instrument discipline: which document is allowed to say what

This is the rule that prevents the worst defects in the whole chain.

| Say this | Only here |
| --- | --- |
| What was delivered and how good it was | Bien ban nghiem thu |
| What files evidence it | Danh muc ho so |
| A change to contract value or scope | Phu luc |
| A change to a payment schedule | Phu luc |
| The amount now payable | Cong van de nghi thanh toan |
| A remediation obligation and its deadline | Bien ban nghiem thu, Dieu 4 |

**The failure mode is a payment request that argues its own adjustments.** It
grows to several pages, and somewhere in it a sentence folds acceptance-driven
deductions into a "cumulative fixed fee", which reads as a permanent reduction of
the contract that nobody agreed to. Once a letter starts explaining why the
number is smaller, the number has become a negotiation and the letter has become
an unsigned amendment. Split it: adjustments to the Phu luc, the net to the
letter.

The mirror failure is a **bien ban that recites remediation the parties have
dropped**. When the minutes stop imposing an obligation, sweep every other
document that referred to it. Two sentences in a payment request survived a
change of position for a full revision cycle this way.

## 8. Dates, numbering, and the response window

- **Sequence the dates to the chain** (section 1), and set the gap by the
  contract, not by the calendar of drafting. If the client has five working days
  to respond to the minutes, the payment request is dated at the end of that
  window. Count working days from the next working day: minutes on a Saturday
  start the count on Monday.
- **A date confirmed earlier stays at its own date.** If a figure was confirmed
  on the 14th, the request issued on the 28th still cites the 14th for that
  figure. Re-dating a document is not re-dating its facts.
- **Every cited annex must carry its date on its own face.** A filename is not a
  date. If a document is cited as "ngay dd/mm/yyyy", open it and confirm the date
  is printed in the document itself.
- **Numbering**: `[nn]/[YYYY]/BBNT/[A]-[B]` for minutes, `Phu luc [nn]` numbered
  in one series across the life of the contract, `So: [nn]/DNTT` for payment
  letters, `[nnnn]/[YYYY]/HDDV/[A]-[B]` for the contract itself. Leave the
  signing date on a document that is not yet signed as `..../..../[year]` rather
  than filling in a hoped-for date.

## 9. Money mechanics

- **Compute, then write.** Build money documents so the arithmetic is asserted
  rather than typed, and reconcile the same figures across the minutes, the
  amendment and the letter. Three documents carrying the same number is three
  chances to be inconsistent.
- **VAT on every line.** A deduction has a pre-VAT amount, a VAT amount, and a
  total. State which of the headline figures are VAT inclusive. Where the rate is
  a temporary reduced rate, say the rate on the face of the document.
- **A percentage self-assessment needs a basis.** If a line is recognised at 71
  per cent, show the blocks and the per-block percentages that produce it. And
  check the line claimed at 100 per cent hardest: it is usually the line with the
  least evidence in the folder.
- **Pass-through costs (chi ho)** are not part of the fixed fee. They settle at
  the final tranche on actual documented cost. Keep them on their own line in
  every document, and never let them enter a percentage calculation.
- **Invoice timing is a contract term.** Whether the VAT invoice issues on
  acceptance or on receipt of payment changes the provider's tax month. Cite the
  clause in the letter rather than assuming.
- Route the tax treatment itself to the accountant; see `tax-compliance.md`.

## 10. Evidence discipline

- **An evidence index must reconcile to the folder.** Row count equals file
  count, or the index is wrong. Regenerate it, do not patch it.
- **Rename evidence before indexing.** Raw camera names carry no date, no
  session and no meaning, and they are what makes a file set look like a dump
  rather than a record.
- **The empty folder is the finding.** A milestone claimed at full value with no
  evidence folder is the first thing a careful counterparty will ask about.
- Evidence folders are shared with the client, so they hold only shareable
  material: no personal data, no internal notes, no cost documents.

## 11. Audience discipline

**The acceptance package and the payment request have different audiences even
though they cover the same milestone.** Where the client is an intermediary, the
acceptance package may be forwarded to the end client; the payment request may
not, because a provider's cost base reveals the intermediary's margin by
subtraction. Keep them in separate folders from the start. The practical benefit
is smaller and immediate: re-dating or rewording the payment letter then touches
nothing inside the shared package.

For the same reason, acceptance documents to a Vietnamese counterparty are
**Vietnamese only**, even where the rest of the engagement is bilingual. Record
that as a deliberate choice so a later consistency pass does not "fix" it.

## 12. Review checklist

- [ ] The chain exists in order and each document cites only documents dated on
      or before itself.
- [ ] Contract number and signing date filled everywhere, not left as a
      placeholder in one copy.
- [ ] Dieu 2 of the minutes states exclusions next to inclusions.
- [ ] Conditional acceptance has either a dated remediation or an explicit
      release; not both open.
- [ ] The deduction clause carries the conditionality sentence tying it to the
      signed Phu luc.
- [ ] Value or scope changes sit in a Phu luc under the amendment clause, never
      in the payment letter.
- [ ] Permanent reduction versus acceptance-driven deduction identified per line.
- [ ] No-double-counting stated where adjusted lines overlap.
- [ ] Payment letter is short, has two numbered blocks, and argues nothing.
- [ ] `Bang chu` matches the digits; VAT stated; pass-through costs on their own
      line and outside the percentages.
- [ ] Deemed-acceptance and surviving-obligations sentences present.
- [ ] Evidence index reconciles to the folder; no personal data or cost material
      inside a package the client may forward.
- [ ] Every document exists in only one authoritative location, and copies
      elsewhere were updated in the same pass.
- [ ] Form and signing space checked against `signed-document-form.md`.
- [ ] Output ends with the not-a-lawyer line and the accountant handoff for
      anything touching VAT, invoicing, or withholding.
