# Legal Updates 2025-2026: What Changed and the Current Citation List

> UNVERIFIED imported reference/template. Legal claims, figures, dates, and
> citations require current primary-source verification before use, as described
> in SKILL.md. Examples and document-style defaults are not universal legal rules.

Read this first on almost any task. Vietnam rewrote its corporate, administrative,
insurance, and tax law inside roughly one year. This file is the filter you run
every document through, plus the current legal-basis ("Can cu") citations to use
in headers.

## 1. Administrative reorganization (two-tier local government)

- Effective **1 July 2025** (Resolution 202/2025/QH15 and related decisions),
  Vietnam consolidated **63 provinces into 34** and **abolished the district level
  (quan / huyen)** entirely.
- Local government is now **two tiers**: provincial (tinh / centrally-run city)
  and commune (xa / phuong / special zone). HCMC and Da Nang remain among the 6
  centrally-run cities.
- **Address format change.** Drop the district line. An address is now
  `[number, street], [Phuong/Xa], [Tinh/Thanh pho], Viet Nam`. Use the address on the
  current registration supplied by the user; verify any administrative mapping.
- Any template still carrying `Quan 3`, `Phuong 7`, `District 3`, or `Q2` in a
  head-office line is stale and will read as pre-July-2025.

## 2. Enterprise Law amendment and the new registration decree

- **Law 76/2025/QH15** (passed 17 June 2025, effective **1 July 2025**) amends the
  Enterprise Law 2020 (59/2020/QH14, previously amended by 03/2022/QH15). Cite the
  Enterprise Law as "Luat Doanh nghiep so 59/2020/QH14 (sua doi, bo sung boi Luat
  so 76/2025/QH15)".
- **Decree 168/2025/ND-CP** (30 June 2025) on enterprise registration **replaced
  Decree 01/2021/ND-CP**. Registration forms and procedures follow Decree 168 now.
- **Business registry authority moved** to the provincial **So Tai chinh**
  (Department of Finance), Phong Doanh nghiep va Dang ky kinh doanh. The old
  "So Ke hoach va Dau tu" label is stale.
- **Beneficial owner (BO) regime.** Companies must collect, store, and on request
  disclose beneficial-owner information (ownership >= 25% or control). A company
  formed **before 1 July 2025 is not required to disclose immediately**; it
  submits the BO list the **next time it registers any change** (business line,
  legal representative, member, capital, etc.). Flag this on any change filing.
- Expanded prohibited acts: false declaration of registered content and inflated
  charter-capital declaration are now explicitly prohibited and defined.
- Cadres/public officials may now establish and manage enterprises in science,
  technology, innovation, and digital-transformation fields (aligned with
  Resolution 193/2025/QH15). Relevant if a public-sector partner wants to
  participate in a venture.
- A new **Law on Investment 143/2025/QH15** was adopted 11 December 2025; relevant
  mainly for foreign investment and incentives, not day-to-day domestic contracts.

## 3. Labor and social insurance

- The **Labor Code 2019 (45/2019/QH14, effective 1 Jan 2021)** is still the
  governing labor law; it was not replaced. Its core rules (two contract types,
  probation, termination, severance) stand. See `labor-hr.md`.
- **Social Insurance Law 2024 (Law 41/2024/QH15, effective 1 July 2025)** replaced
  the 2014 law. Detailed by Decree 158/2025/ND-CP.
  - Total statutory social contribution is **32% of the contribution base**:
    employer **21.5%** (SI 17.5%, HI 3%, UI 1%) and employee **10.5%** (SI 8%,
    HI 1.5%, UI 1%).
  - **Unemployment insurance (UI)** applies to Vietnamese employees only; foreign
    employees with valid work permits pay SI and HI but are exempt from UI.
  - Contribution cap for SI and HI is **20x the reference/base level = VND
    46,800,000/month** (2026 figure).
  - Board members / legal representatives who take **dividends rather than a
    salary** sit under a different contribution arrangement; confirm before
    assuming standard payroll contributions apply.
- **Trade Union Law 2024 (effective 1 July 2025):** employer contributes 2% of
  payroll to the trade-union fund; where a grassroots union exists, employees
  contribute 1% capped at VND 180,000/month.
- **Regional minimum wage from 1 January 2026 (Decree 293/2025/ND-CP):** Region I
  VND 5,310,000; Region II VND 4,730,000; Region III VND 4,140,000; Region IV
  VND 3,700,000. Region is set by where the company/branch operates, not where the
  employee lives. HCMC urban core and central Da Nang are Region I. This is a
  floor for both wage and the SI contribution base.

## 4. Personal income tax reform (2026)

- **Law 109/2025/QH15** reforms PIT, largely applicable from the **2026 tax year**
  (a few items from 1 July 2026).
  - Personal deduction (giam tru gia canh) raised to **VND 15,500,000/month**;
    dependent deduction **VND 6,200,000/month** per registered dependent.
  - Resident progressive schedule simplified from **7 brackets to 5** (still
    5% to 35%). Non-residents: flat **20%** on Vietnam-sourced income.
  - Practical effect: a resident with one dependent has no PIT until monthly
    income exceeds roughly VND 28,600,000 after compulsory insurance.
- **Service-contract (HDDV) withholding is unchanged in mechanism:** payments to a
  non-employee individual **over VND 2,000,000** per payment are subject to **10%
  PIT withholding** by the paying company, credited at annual finalization. See
  `tax-compliance.md`.

## 5. The current "Can cu" citation list

Use these exact anchors in legal-basis chains. Drop anything not relevant to the
document.

- Bo luat Dan su so 91/2015/QH13 (Civil Code 2015). Do NOT cite the 2005 Civil
  Code (33/2005/QH11); it is superseded. Older templates in the archive still cite
  it, which is a giveaway of a stale form.
- Luat Thuong mai so 36/2005/QH11 (Commercial Law 2005), for commercial service
  contracts.
- Luat Doanh nghiep so 59/2020/QH14, sua doi bo sung boi Luat so 76/2025/QH15
  (Enterprise Law), plus Nghi dinh 168/2025/ND-CP for registration.
- Bo luat Lao dong so 45/2019/QH14 (Labor Code 2019), for anything labor.
- Luat Bao hiem xa hoi so 41/2024/QH15 (Social Insurance Law 2024) and Nghi dinh
  158/2025/ND-CP, for social-insurance clauses.
- Luat Thue thu nhap ca nhan (PIT) as amended by Luat so 109/2025/QH15, for PIT
  clauses.
- Dieu le cong ty (the company's own charter) and the relevant internal decision,
  for corporate governance acts.

## 6. Quick staleness checklist

Run any inherited template against these. Each hit means the template predates the
2025-2026 rewrite and needs updating:

- [ ] Address contains a district ("Quan", "Huyen", "District", "Q2/Q3").
- [ ] Header or filing names "So Ke hoach va Dau tu" instead of "So Tai chinh".
- [ ] "Can cu" chain cites the **2005 Civil Code** (33/2005/QH11).
- [ ] Enterprise Law cited as 59/2020 with **no** 76/2025 amendment.
- [ ] Social-insurance or salary figures based on pre-July-2025 rates or a
      pre-2026 minimum wage.
- [ ] Letterhead does not match the current company
      registration.
- [ ] Decree 01/2021/ND-CP cited for registration instead of 168/2025/ND-CP.
