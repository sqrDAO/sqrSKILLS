# Commercial Contracts: Service, Consulting, Cooperation, NDA

> UNVERIFIED imported reference/template. Legal claims, figures, dates, and

Covers the day-to-day commercial paper: service and consulting contracts,
cooperation agreements and MOUs (including with state bodies), and
confidentiality. Vietnamese commercial contracts rest mainly on the Civil Code
2015 and the Commercial Law 2005.

## 1. Service contract (HDDV) with an individual

Use for a discrete, project-scoped engagement of an individual. Structure:
- State header, title `HOP DONG DICH VU`, number `.../2026/HDDV`, place and date.
- "Can cu": Civil Code 2015 (91/2015/QH13); the parties' needs and capacity.
- Ben A (company: name, address in two-tier format, MST, legal rep). Ben B
  (individual: name, DOB, CCCD, permanent address, all as blanks).
- Scope of work (often via an attached Phu luc / appendix listing tasks).
- Term with a notice period to stop (example: 30 days' notice, subject to the agreed terms).
- Fee and payment: monthly amount, transfer method, pay date, and the PIT
  treatment. Note that a fee stated "including PIT" means applicable withholding is
  carved out of that number; a fee stated net means the company grosses up. Be
  explicit which it is (see `tax-compliance.md`).
- Obligations and rights of each party; quality and remedy on complaint.
- Confidentiality (survives termination).
- General clauses: amendment in writing, dispute resolution (negotiation then the
  competent court), 02 originals of equal value.

**Watch the classification line.** If the "service" is really a standing,
supervised role, an HDDV understates cost and creates re-characterization risk;
see `labor-hr.md` section 7 and raise the labor-contract alternative.

## 2. Consulting service contract (bilingual, business-to-business)

Use for the company providing (or receiving) advisory services to/from another
company, often cross-border. Structure mirrors the HDDV but between two
companies, bilingual VN/EN, and typically adds:
- "Can cu": Civil Code 2015 and Commercial Law 2005 (36/2005/QH11).
- A defined **Service Package** (for example investor mapping, fundraising
  materials, financial model, advisory through a closing), scoped to a period
  after which it is deemed complete.
- **Service charge** as a fixed VND fee or a percentage of transaction value,
  stating VAT inclusion and the verified service/date-specific rate; payment window
  (example: within 7 days of invoice, subject to the agreed terms).
- If success-fee-based, define "transaction value" precisely and whether staged
  transactions are billed pro rata.
- Reimbursable costs (legal, research, due diligence, travel) if any, with caps.
- Confidentiality, IP ownership of deliverables, and a governing-language clause
  (Vietnamese governs, English is a mirror).

If the user provides a consulting-service-contract precedent, update its
letterhead, registration, address format, and verified "Can cu" citations.

## 3. Cooperation agreement / MOU

**Thoa thuan hop tac** sets a non-binding-to-lightly-binding framework for
collaboration (co-organizing events, cross-referral, joint programs). Structure:
- State header, title `THOA THUAN HOP TAC`, place and date.
- Recitals ("Can cu") that can include the relevant resolutions and decisions
  underpinning the collaboration, especially with public partners.
- Both parties (name, representative, title, address, phone).
- Purpose of cooperation.
- Content of cooperation: the concrete activities each side will do (for
  example events, training, hackathons, advisory, market/investment
  connection, co-organizing and media support).
- Funding: often "from each party's lawful sources," with no committed figure.
- Implementation: focal points, periodic leadership meetings.
- Confidentiality, term (for example 2 years), and effect on signing.
- Made in 02 originals of equal value.

Keep an MOU deliberately non-binding on money unless the user wants a binding
commitment; if binding obligations are intended, say so and tighten the language.

## 4. Cooperation with a state body or public unit

When the counterparty is a public innovation body or other state unit, check:
- Recitals should cite the governing resolutions and decisions that authorize the
  public partner (national startup-ecosystem decisions, provincial innovation
  decisions, the reorganization decision that created or renamed the unit).
  Getting these citations current matters more with public partners.
- Confirm the correct current name and address of the public unit after the 2025
  reorganization; units were merged and renamed.
- Funding language typically references the public partner's "science-and-
  technology non-business budget" plus the company's own funds and other lawful
  sources.
- Keep confidentiality mutual and note the carve-out for disclosures required of a
  state body by law.

## 5. Confidentiality (NDA and NDA clauses)

Whether standalone or a clause, a Vietnamese-practice confidentiality provision
defines confidential information broadly (technical, commercial, financial,
customer, product, roadmap), obliges non-copying, non-disclosure, and non-use,
requires return on request, and **survives termination**. For a mutual NDA,
mirror the obligations both ways. Add carve-outs for information that is public,
already known, independently developed, or compelled by law.

## 6. Clauses that carry the weight

- **Governing language.** Vietnamese governs; English is a faithful mirror. State
  this so a translation mismatch does not become a dispute.
- **Payment and tax.** Be explicit on VAT (inclusive/exclusive) and on PIT/foreign
  contractor tax where an individual or a foreign party is paid; see
  `tax-compliance.md`.
- **Term and termination.** Fixed term or open with a notice period; state the
  notice days and any early-termination consequences.
- **Dispute resolution.** Negotiation first, then a named competent court (or
  arbitration such as VIAC if the parties prefer and the matter is commercial).
- **Force majeure, confidentiality, IP, and assignment.** Include as fits the
  deal.
- **Originals and signatures.** 02 originals of equal value; company signs with
  legal representative and seal.

## 7. Drafting for the close-out you will have to run

Most disputes on a service contract are not about the contract, they are about
getting a milestone accepted and paid. The clauses below are what make that
possible, and their absence is what makes it painful. Draft them in; if you are
reviewing someone else's contract, look for them by name. The documents they
generate are covered in `contract-execution.md`.

- **A scope appendix with acceptance criteria per line.** Not a list of
  activities: a table of deliverable, quantity, and the basis on which each line
  is confirmed (documents attached, client confirmation on site, a measured
  output). This appendix is what the acceptance minutes reconcile against line by
  line, so a vague appendix guarantees a vague acceptance.
- **Payment tranches tied to named milestones, with percentages and amounts.**
  State which event triggers which tranche and what the tranche is worth as both
  a percentage and a figure.
- **An acceptance procedure clause.** Who requests acceptance, what file set has
  to be handed over, how many working days the client has to respond in writing,
  and what happens on silence. **Always negotiate a deemed-acceptance sentence**:
  without it, a client who simply does not reply can stall a tranche
  indefinitely.
- **A remedy clause** giving the client a route to require rework rather than to
  withhold payment: what a defect notice must contain, how long the provider has,
  and what happens after.
- **An amendment clause** stating that any change to value, scope or schedule is
  made by a written phu luc signed by both parties. This is the clause every
  later adjustment will hang off, so give it a number you can cite.
- **An invoice-timing clause.** Whether the VAT invoice issues on acceptance, on
  the request, or on receipt of the money. This decides the provider's tax month
  and is not something to leave to practice.
- **A pass-through cost clause (chi ho).** If the provider pays third parties on
  the client's behalf, say so, say that these are reimbursed at documented actual
  cost, and say which tranche settles them. Keep them outside the fixed fee so
  they never enter a percentage calculation.
- **A partial-performance clause.** If a line is not performed or is performed
  below standard, say how the fee is adjusted. A contract that only contemplates
  full performance turns every shortfall into a negotiation.
- **A surviving-obligations clause.** Confidentiality, IP, data, source files,
  latent defects, and the post-engagement report survive acceptance of any
  milestone. Say it, so signing the minutes does not read as a full discharge.

## 8. Review checklist

- [ ] Correct contract type for the substance (service vs consulting vs MOU vs
      labor); classification risk checked for individual "service" engagements.
- [ ] Header, number series, place/date correct; bilingual with a Vietnamese-
      governs clause.
- [ ] "Can cu" chain current (Civil Code 2015, Commercial Law 2005, not the 2005
      Civil Code).
- [ ] Parties' legal names, MST, and addresses current;
      letterhead matches the confirmed registration.
- [ ] Fee clause states VAT and PIT/withholding treatment unambiguously
      (inclusive vs gross-up).
- [ ] Scope defined or attached as an appendix; deliverables and completion clear.
- [ ] Confidentiality survives termination; carve-outs present.
- [ ] Dispute resolution and originals clauses present.
- [ ] Personal data left as blanks; only confirmed transaction parties named.
- [ ] Scope appendix carries acceptance criteria per line, not just activities.
- [ ] Payment tranches tied to named milestones with percentages and figures.
- [ ] Acceptance procedure clause with a response window and deemed acceptance.
- [ ] Amendment clause present and citable; partial-performance treatment stated.
- [ ] Invoice-timing and pass-through cost clauses explicit.
- [ ] Surviving obligations listed so acceptance does not read as full discharge.
- [ ] Signing space and form checked against `signed-document-form.md`.
- [ ] Ends with the not-a-lawyer line and any tax handoff to the accountant.
