## 1. What it is, and what it is not

- **Full title:** Nghi quyet so 24/2026/NQ-HĐND ngay 29/5/2026 cua HĐND thanh pho
  Da Nang quy dinh cac linh vuc uu tien; tieu chi, dieu kien, trinh tu, thu tuc,
  noi dung hoat dong khoi nghiep doi moi sang tao (KNDMST), hoat dong ho tro
  KNDMST, hoat dong trong linh vuc thiet ke, san xuat, dong goi, kiem thu vi mach
  ban dan, tri tue nhan tao duoc mien thue tren dia ban thanh pho Da Nang.
- **Effective:** 10 June 2026.
- **Replaces:** Nghi quyet 53/2024/NQ-HĐND and 59/2024/NQ-HĐND, both of
  13 December 2024 (Dieu 23.1). A dossier or template citing either is stale.
- **Why it exists:** Resolution 136/2024/QH15 (Da Nang's special-mechanism
  resolution) granted tax exemptions but set no criteria or procedure for deciding
  who qualifies. NQ 24 supplies the criteria, forms and process for the Da Nang
  **So Khoa hoc va Cong nghe** (Department of Science and Technology, DOST) to
  issue a confirmation letter.
- **The output is a confirmation letter, not money and not a tax decision**
  (Dieu 16.1, 16.2; 17.7; 18.7). DOST does not decide how much tax is exempted.
  With the letter in hand, the entity still works the exemption through the
  Da Nang tax authority under tax law (Dieu 20.3; 21.1).

## 2. Where the benefit itself lives

NQ 24 states no tax rate, tax type or exemption period. Look these up in the
national resolutions before stating any number:

| Beneficiary | Benefit source | NQ 24 article |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Innovative startups and startup-support organizations | Resolution 136/2024/QH15 | Dieu 16.2.a |
| Semiconductor and AI enterprises | Resolution 136/2024/QH15 as amended by Resolution 259/2025/QH15 | Dieu 16.2.b |
| Experts, scientists and talented individuals | Resolution 136/2024/QH15, Dieu 14.1.c | Dieu 15.1; 20.1 |

The council member allowances in Dieu 19.3 (VND 1,200,000 / 1,000,000 / 300,000 /
200,000 per person per session) are budget spending on the appraisal, not a
benefit to the applicant. Do not present them as one.

## 3. Who can apply, and who cannot

Two filing routes, plus two groups that do not file at all.

**Procedure A (Dieu 17): startups and support organizations**
- Innovative startups (doanh nghiep khoi nghiep sang tao) with head office in
  Da Nang, operating no more than five years (Dieu 2.1; 11).
- Support organizations: science and technology organizations, the Startup Support
  Centre, startup-support intermediaries (Dieu 2.2; 12).

**Procedure B (Dieu 18): semiconductor and AI enterprises**
- B1: enterprises designing, manufacturing, packaging or testing semiconductors
  (Dieu 2.1; 13).
- B2: enterprises with AI activities (Dieu 2.1; 14).

**No filing route of their own**
- Individuals (experts, scientists, startup staff, semiconductor and AI staff):
  the employer identifies them, keeps the evidence file, and produces it on
  request (Dieu 2.4; 15; 20.1). Individuals do not file with DOST.
- Transferors of shares, capital contributions or contribution rights into a
  startup, semiconductor or AI enterprise (Dieu 2.3).

**Excluded**
- Individuals doing only administrative, finance, accounting or HR work, or work
  that does not directly create the product, service or technology (Dieu 6;
  repeated at 15.1.a and 15.2.b).
- Companies that only assemble products: assembly does not count as semiconductor
  manufacturing (Dieu 7.2.e).
- Startups more than five years past their first enterprise registration
  certificate (GCN DKDN) (Dieu 11.1).

## 4. Mandatory conditions

1. **Every applicant:** lawfully established, head office in Da Nang
   (Dieu 11.1; 12.1; 13.2; 14.2).
2. **Startup:** no more than five years from first GCN DKDN; startup activity in a
   priority field under Dieu 3, **or** a startup certificate (Giay cong nhan doanh
   nghiep khoi nghiep sang tao) under Decree 268/2025/ND-CP (Dieu 11.1; 11.2).
3. **Support organization:** lawfully established with head office in Da Nang and
   carrying out startup-support activities under Dieu 5 (Dieu 12.1; 12.2).
4. **Semiconductor enterprise, all four:** activity under Dieu 7; matching
   registered business lines; staff under Dieu 9; equipment investment **or**
   revenue (Dieu 13.1 to 13.4).
5. **AI enterprise, all four:** activity under Dieu 8; matching business lines;
   staff under Dieu 10; infrastructure investment, revenue **or** output products
   (Dieu 14.1 to 14.4).
6. **Individual:** a labor contract or hiring decision from a confirmed entity,
   stating position and duties, with a job description attached; an expert must
   meet at least one of six criteria (Dieu 15.1.a; 15.1.b; 15.2.b; 15.3).

**Priority fields (Dieu 3)** point to four external lists. Check the applicant's
activity against them before filing: QD 21/2026/QD-TTg, QD 38/2020/QD-TTg,
QD 2117/QD-TTg and QD 3345/QD-UBND (numbers as given in the deck; verify each).

## 5. Dossier checklists

One set per procedure. All six forms sit inside the resolution itself: Appendix I
(forms I-01 to I-03) and Appendix II (forms II-01 to II-03).

**Procedure A (Dieu 17.3)**
- [ ] Application for confirmation, original, signed and sealed, **Form I-01** (17.3.a)
- [ ] GCN DKDN, copy. DOST pulls it from the national database; it is required
      only when that lookup fails (17.3.b)
- [ ] Activity description (thuyet minh), original, **Form I-02** (17.3.c)
- [ ] Supporting evidence, if any, certified copies: IP titles, decisions
      recognizing research results, award certificates (17.3.d)

**Procedure B1, semiconductor (Dieu 18.3.a)**
- [ ] Application, original, signed and sealed by the legal representative, **Form II-01**
- [ ] Contracts with design, manufacturing, packaging or testing staff, **with payroll records**
- [ ] Evidence of activity under Dieu 7
- [ ] Evidence of investment in, or aid or donation of, equipment, software or production lines

**Procedure B2, AI (Dieu 18.3.b)**
- [ ] Application including an undertaking not to infringe software copyright, **Form II-02**
- [ ] Staff contracts **with payroll records**
- [ ] Evidence of activity under Dieu 8
- [ ] Evidence of infrastructure or data investment, revenue, or AI output products
- [ ] Copyright or IP certificates, if any

**Form I-02 content.** The description must name the Dieu 3 priority field the
activity falls in (footnote 6 of the form). Per the deck, footnote 7 mentions
revenue growth of at least 20% over two consecutive years. Until the signed text is
checked, treat this as a reporting item: where the applicant has two years of
revenue, report both there; do not present the 20% test as an eligibility or filing
requirement, and do not tell a pre-revenue startup it cannot file (section 12).
Forms I-03 and II-03 are the confirmation letters DOST issues; the applicant does
not fill them in.

## 6. Where and how to file

DOST receives, appraises and confirms under both procedures (Dieu 16.1; 17.6;
18.6). The enterprise or organization files in its own name (Dieu 17.1.a;
18.1.a). Three channels (Dieu 17.4.a; 18.4.a):

1. **In person:** the Da Nang city public administration service centre (Trung tam
   phuc vu hanh chinh cong), or a ward or commune service centre.
2. **Public postal service:** addressed to the city service centre.
3. **Online:** the National Public Service Portal (Cong Dich vu cong quoc gia).

Results come back through the service centres (Dieu 17.4.b; 18.4.b).

## 7. Appraisal council and criteria

| Item | Rule | Article |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | One file-appraisal council (Hoi dong tham dinh ho so), shared by both procedures | 19; 17.1.c; 18.1.c |
| Established by | Director of DOST | 19.1.a |
| Size | 5 to 7 members: chair, one deputy chair if any, members; plus a secretary | 19.1.a |
| Quorum | At least 2/3 of members present, including the chair or deputy | 19.1.c |
| Adopting a conclusion | At least 3/4 of members present agree, open vote or secret ballot | 19.1.c |
| Meeting | In person, online or combined | 19.1.c |
| Sequence | Agree the rules, appraise, may question the applicant, conclude, adopt minutes | 19.1.đ |
| Outcome | Agreed or not agreed. **No scoring scale, score sheet or pass mark** | 19.1.đ |

Members must appraise honestly and impartially and keep what they receive
confidential (19.1.b).

**Two layers are checked (19.1.d):** is the activity of the right type, and does
the applicant meet the legal criteria. The pairing per applicant:

- Startup: Dieu 3 + Dieu 4 + Dieu 11
- Support organization: Dieu 5 + Dieu 12
- Semiconductor enterprise: Dieu 7 + Dieu 13
- AI enterprise: Dieu 8 + Dieu 14

Content outside Dieu 9 and 10 is determined by DOST case by case (9.3.d; 10.4).

## 8. Processing time

All periods are **working days**, running from DOST's receipt of a complete and
valid file (Dieu 17.1.b; 18.1.b).

- **Procedure A: 21 working days total** (Dieu 17.5) = 15 working days for DOST to
  establish the council + 6 working days from the meeting to issue the
  confirmation or a reasoned refusal (Dieu 17.1.c).
- **Procedure B: 10 working days total** (Dieu 18.5), covering council set-up, the
  appraisal meeting and issuance (Dieu 18.1.c).

If the database lookup of the GCN DKDN fails, DOST asks for a copy (17.3.b), which
presumably holds up a "complete and valid" file. Confirm how that affects the
start date.

## 9. Who signs what

The Director of DOST signs, **not** the Chairman of the City People's Committee.

| Document | Signed by | Article |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision establishing the appraisal council | Director, DOST | 19.1.a |
| Confirmation of startup or support activity, Form I-03 | Director, DOST | 17.7; Appendix I |
| Confirmation of semiconductor or AI enterprise, Form II-03 | Director, DOST | 18.7; Appendix II |
| Written reply giving reasons for refusal | DOST | 17.1.c; 18.1.c |
| Tax exemption, declaration and finalization after confirmation | Da Nang tax authority (Thue thanh pho Da Nang) | 20.3; 21.1 |

## 10. After confirmation

- Take the confirmation letter to the tax authority; the exemption is claimed and
  finalized under tax law, with the accountant (Dieu 20.3; 21.1).
- For individuals, the employer keeps the evidence file (contract or hiring
  decision, position, duties, job description) and produces it on request (20.1).
- **Notify DOST** when a confirmed activity is paused or stops (Dieu 20.4).

## 11. Five common dossier mistakes

1. A startup filing more than five years after its first GCN DKDN (Dieu 11.1).
2. A semiconductor or AI file with staff contracts but no payroll records
   (Dieu 18.3.a; 18.3.b).
3. A contract or hiring decision that does not state the position and duties, with
   no job description attached (Dieu 15.1.a; 15.2.b; 15.3).
4. An assembly-only electronics firm declaring itself a semiconductor enterprise
   (Dieu 7.2.e).
5. Not notifying DOST when confirmed activities pause or stop (Dieu 20.4).

## 12. Open questions and inconsistencies

- **Form 04 does not exist.** Per the deck, Dieu 17.1.c refers to a confirmation
  letter "theo mau so 04 Phu luc I", but Appendix I lists only Forms I-01, I-02 and
  I-03. Treat Form I-03 as the confirmation letter and flag the mismatch; do not
  invent a Form 04.
- **Form I-02 footnote 7 (20% revenue growth).** It sits in a form footnote. Check
  whether the articles make it a condition for all applicants or only a reporting
  item before telling a pre-revenue startup it cannot qualify.
- **Priority-field lists.** The four decisions named in section 4 were not checked
  against their own text. Confirm the numbers and which list governs the activity.
- **Rates and periods.** Only Resolutions 136/2024/QH15 and 259/2025/QH15 carry
  them. This skill has not verified either.

## 13. How to use this file

- **Explain (Job C):** answer who qualifies, which procedure, what to file, how
  long, who signs. Say plainly that the output is a letter, not money and not a
  tax amount.
- **Review a dossier (Job B):** walk sections 4 and 5 item by item, then section 11.
  Flag any citation of NQ 53/2024 or 59/2024 as stale.
- **Draft (Job A):** Forms I-01, I-02, II-01 and II-02 are in the resolution's
  appendices; start from the official form, not a skeleton. In the I-02
  description, name the Dieu 3 priority field; give two years of revenue where
  the applicant has them, without treating the 20% growth note as a condition
  (section 12).
- **Structure (Job D):** for a company doing both AI and startup activity, compare
  Procedure A (21 working days) with Procedure B2 (10 working days) and the
  benefit each unlocks under section 2.
- **Handoff:** DOST for the confirmation itself; the accountant for the exemption
  claim with the tax authority; a licensed lawyer if eligibility turns on a
  contested reading of Dieu 3, 7 or 8.
